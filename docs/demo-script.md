# 7-minute interview demo

1. Register a creator profile with an initial connected platform account (or use the seeded creator).
2. Show the connected-account security score.
3. Select a coverage limit of ₹150,000, run a quote and bind the standard quote. A new creator's lower security score affects the indicative premium but does not block standard terms.
4. Open BillingCenter Simulator, pay the invoice and confirm the policy becomes active.
5. Report an account takeover and enter the claimed recovery, legal and revenue amounts.
6. Open the claim as an adjuster and review its exposures.
7. Add evidence references as the creator. Verify each item as an adjuster and record the amount supported by financial documents.
8. Run AI investigation and show its triage, verified-evidence and risk outputs.
9. Run deterministic coverage evaluation and loss assessment; explain waiting period, sublimits and deductible.
10. Request information, deny or approve with a reason. AI cannot make the final decision.
11. Process an approved claim payment, close the claim and inspect the audit trail.

Closing line:

"The same domain boundaries can be mapped to a licensed Guidewire environment later; the simulator gives us a testable local representation of PolicyCenter, ClaimCenter and BillingCenter without pretending we have licensed access."
